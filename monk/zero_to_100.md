# Comfortably raising a monk from 0 to 100

This guide takes a new monk from character creation to level 100 as fast as comfort allows, and shows what the character is worth on the Char Bazaar at the end. It builds on the [monk guide](guide.md): the same comfort grades (only hunting grounds with Prep and Hunt of 0 or 1), the same hunting grounds and the same model. It assumes a premium account. Every number is an estimate; the last part explains how they're made.

**In short:** hunt 3 hours a day and train offline 12 hours a day. Go through Targuna, then follow the route below with a Stealth Ring, ending in Corym Mines, and do the free Any Creature kill task and only the cheap delivery tasks. That's level 100 in about **28 days** (84 hours of hunting), with fist fighting about 78. A monk like that has sold for a median of about **251 TC**.

## What the plan assumes
- **3 hours of hunting a day.** They're all in green stamina (+50% experience): stamina refills offline, and 21 hours offline a day is more than the 18 hours its last 3 hours need.
- **12 hours of offline training every day**, at a Training Statue with Fist Fighting + Shielding (the Adventurers' Guild, the Blue Valley temple, or Targuna's, which lets you pick). Your training time refills one second for every second you're online or offline without training, so 3 hours online plus 9 hours offline gives the next day's 12 hours back.
- **Skill only from offline training.** Hunting adds a little more, so the real skill will be a bit higher.
- **No deaths, no bought experience boosts and no events.** Deaths make it slower; events and boosts make it faster.
- **The hunting grounds come from the monk guide's Good for XP lists** (Section 1, and Section 1a with the ring): each day, the best one for your level and skill.

## Day 0: create the character
1. **Pick the world first:** a world with Optional PvP and green BattlEye, in South America or North America. It decides the price later (see "Selling the character"), and moving later costs a world transfer.
2. **On Newhaven, the starting island**, do the [Newhaven Quest](https://tibia.fandom.com/wiki/Newhaven_Quest) (1,600 experience and a Guardian Ring) and the [Newhaven Legion Helmet Quest](https://tibia.fandom.com/wiki/Newhaven_Legion_Helmet_Quest) before you leave. You can't go back.
3. **On Targuna, at level 8, train offline first:** use the Training Statue and log out for 12 hours. That takes fist fighting from about 16 to 43 and shielding from 12 to 27, which more than doubles your damage.

## Weekly tasks: only the cheap ones
The **Task Board** gives each character 6 delivery tasks and 6 kill tasks a week, reset on Monday at server save (9 of each with the Permanent Weekly Task Expansion, 750 TC in the Store). The list is drawn for you: you can't choose or reroll it. A delivery task gives **half a level of experience up to level 82**, and 1,994 × your level after that. Kill tasks give the same, with a cap (200k experience for beginner tasks, 800k for adept).

**One kill task is free: "Any Creature", 1,000 kills of anything.** Your hunting gets there in the first days of each week, on any route (kills of summons don't count). The timeline below counts it.

The catch is the delivery items. Each delivery asks for 5 to 500 of an item drawn from 484 possible ones, mostly creature products, some armors and weapons; the amount is random within the item's own range (Swarmer Antennas 50–100, Silver Poniards 10–20). At Market prices the middle amount costs about 191k gold for half of them, and only 5% cost less than 20k. At low levels, half a level is worth little, so most deliveries cost more than the hunting they save.

The rule: **hand in a delivery only when its items cost less than what you'd loot in the time its experience saves.** On the ring route, that means:

| Level | Experience per delivery | Hunting time it replaces | Worth paying up to | Tasks that cheap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30 | 20k | 10 min | 2k | 0% |
| 40 | 37k | 21 min | 7k | 0% |
| 50 | 59k | 34 min | 6k | 0% |
| 60 | 86k | 37 min | 102k | 38% |
| 70 | 117k | 48 min | 141k | 43% |
| 80 | 154k | 35 min | 63k | 28% |
| 90 | 179k | 35 min | 95k | 36% |
| 100 | 199k | 39 min | 105k | 38% |

- **Deliver on Sunday,** the last day before the reset: the experience depends on your level when you hand it in, so later pays more.
- **Keep what your hunting ground drops** if one of the week's deliveries asks for it: that one is free.
- **Check the price on your world's Market** before buying; it can be very different from the numbers above (Antica, November 2026).
- **The other kill tasks** ask for few kills of easy creatures (24 to 56 in a sample week). One gives the same experience as a delivery, so it's worth the "Hunting time it replaces" in the table above. Do it when the creature lives where you already hunt (a Stonerefiner task is free experience). Go somewhere else for one only when the trip and the kills take less than that time: almost never at low levels, often near level 100 for an easy creature close to a boat or depot. The timeline counts only the Any Creature task.
- **Keep Task Difficulty at Beginner** (on the Bounty Tasks tab): its 200k experience cap never binds before level 100 (a task gives 199,401 there), and harder difficulties bring harder creatures.
- **Ignore the rest of the Task Board while raising.** Bounty tasks give little experience (800 to 2,300 at Beginner in a sample), and the Bounty Talisman's bonuses work only against the current bounty creature. Hunting Task Points and Soulseals, paid after the week, buy outfits, mounts, Promotion Points and Soulpit fights, not experience.

## Prey: why it barely helps here
The prey system (Ctrl+Y) gives a 2-hour bonus against one creature: a damage boost (7–25%), damage reduction (12–30%), bonus experience (13–40%) or improved loot (13–40%). Premium accounts have 2 slots. Each slot offers 9 random creatures; the list rerolls for free every 20 hours, or for 150 gold × your level. The bonus type is random when you pick.

On this route it doesn't pay:
- **Stonerefiners never appear in the random lists.** They're one of the creatures you can only pick with 5 **Prey Wildcards** ("select a creature"), and that lasts 2 hours. Locking a creature and its bonus also costs 5 wildcards.
- **Wildcards are scarce:** a premium account gets 3 a week from the daily reward (day 3 of the reward lane). The Store sells 5 for 50 TC, which is more than the character earns in those 2 hours.
- **Damage reduction is useless with the ring,** since the monsters that can't see you never hit you. The Infernoids of the Crimson Court can't be picked at all.

So: save the 3 free wildcards each week. Every second week, spend 5 on Stonerefiner. If the bonus isn't experience, one more wildcard rerolls it: it always raises the bonus a step and may change its type (at the top step, it always changes). That's 2 hours of +13–40% experience every two weeks, a small extra the timeline doesn't count.

## Other free boosts
- **Daily reward:** day 7 of the reward lane gives 30 minutes of 50% more experience. Use it on a Corym Mines day. Collecting every day also gives stamina regeneration in depots and temples.
- **Boosted creature:** if the day's boosted creature lives on your route, hunt it that day (twice the experience and loot).
- **Double experience events** happen a few times a year and speed everything up a lot.
- **Not worth it on this route:** imbuements and the Wheel of Destiny (you take almost no damage with the ring), charms (a few percent of damage) and the Store's XP Boost (it costs more Tibia Coins than the character earns while it lasts).

Compared side by side:
- **Raising to 100:** 28 days and 84 hours of play, for about 312 TC (the sale plus the gold left). About 11.1 TC per day.
- **A level 8 trained to fist 110:** about 669 days of training, for about 1697 TC. That's 2.54 TC per day, about 4 times less, but it needs only about 11 hours of logins in total.

So raising is the income, and a trained level 8 is a passive extra on the side:
- **Train several at once.** Characters on one account can train offline at the same time, each with its own daily login, and they all share the account's premium. On the account you already use for raising, they cost no extra premium.
- **Space out the sales.** An account can have only one auction at a time.
- **Don't keep training the level 100.** At levels 90 to 110, monks with skill 100–109 sold for a median of 251 TC (100 sales), barely more than the 221 TC of skill 76–81, so hundreds of days of extra training don't pay.
- **Low-level prices rest on few sales** (3–55 per skill), and they're best in South America.

## How the numbers are made
- **The route:** a day-by-day simulation with the monk guide's model (its appendix) at the character's real level and skill. XP × the low level bonus × green stamina; skill from offline training only, with TibiaWiki's formulas (15 fist points a minute). The monk model hasn't been checked against monk hunts yet: its loot comes from the knights' tests, so treat the route as a first estimate.
- **Weekly tasks:** the Any Creature task once the week's modelled kills reach 1,000; deliveries from TibiaWiki's Delivery Task list, with Market prices from tibiamarket.top (Antica, November 2026).
- **Prices:** sold auctions on exiva.pro.

# Hunting ground tests

Real hunts, used to check and correct the estimates in the guide. Add one row per hunt; tested hunting grounds get a ✓ in the guide and use these numbers instead of estimates.

How to fill each column:
- **Character:** level and fist fighting (for example "level 45, fist 72"). No names needed.
- **Setup:** the guide section you hunted with: 1, 1a (Stealth Ring), 1b (virtues, shrines and more spells), 2 (imbuements), 3 (charms and prey) or 4 (Wheel).
- **Time:** how long the hunt lasted, from leaving the depot to coming back (for example 0h45).
- **Raw XP/h:** the Hunt Analyser's raw XP gain per hour (without stamina, prey or other bonuses).
- **Damage/h:** the Hunt Analyser's damage per hour. It shows your hunting pace: pulls, walking and waiting for respawns.
- **Raw XP per damage:** raw XP gain ÷ damage. It doesn't depend on your pace, so it checks the monster mix the guide assumes. Put the guide's value in brackets if you know it.
- **Loot/h:** what you really made per hour after selling on the Market (bank balance after minus before, per hour). The Hunt Analyser's loot is often much lower, because it uses shop prices; write it in Notes if you want.
- **Healing/h:** the Hunt Analyser's healing per hour. Close to zero means a calm hunt.
- **Supplies:** gold spent on potions and other supplies for the whole hunt.
- **Notes:** kills per creature, the most valuable drops, anything that made the hunt less comfortable (dangerous monsters, summons, too few monsters, other players), and the boosted creature or prey if they applied.

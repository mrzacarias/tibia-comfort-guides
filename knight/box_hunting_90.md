# Box hunting at 90+ (Elite Knight, Nov 2026)

This guide is for a solo Elite Knight from level 90 up who wants **more XP than normal hunting, still without potions**: let a few monsters close around you (a **box**) and hit them all with area spells, while two Basic Void imbuements turn your hits back into mana. Normal hunting, one monster at a time, is in the [main knight guide](guide.md) (below 100) and [Comfortable solo hunting at 100+](comfort_solo_100.md); every table here compares against them.

The numbers come from the same model as those guides, checked against two tested box hunts (see How far to trust them). Log your box hunts in [spot_tests.md](spot_tests.md).

## The short version
- The best box XP per hour, raw: about **117k at 90, 126k at 100, 244k at 200, 258k at 300, 260k at 400, 473k at 500, 589k at 600, 751k at 800**. The best normal hunting grounds make about 103k at 90, 110k at 100, 256k at 300, 547k at 500, 662k at 800.
- **No potions** in the XP and gold lists: every listed hunting ground balances on regeneration, leech and `exura ico`. Carry a few health potions for a bad moment.
- **Box with potions:** each band also lists where letting potions pay for the full rotation gives the most XP, with potions at most a quarter of the loot: about **114k at 90, 120k at 100, 608k at 200, 710k at 300, 754k at 400, 1.1M at 500, 1.1M at 600, 1.6M at 800** raw XP an hour. Tested: a level 494 knight on Edron Vampire Crypt's floor -4 made 501k raw XP an hour and kept about 180k an hour after 31k of potions.
- The area button: **`exori scu` > `exori` > `exori min`** (Shield Slam, Berserk, Front Sweep). Shield Slam is the cheapest (90 mana), hits all eight squares around you and halves the next hit of every monster it hits, so it goes first; most boxes die after one or two casts, so the first spell does most of the work. No `exori gran`: its 360 mana a cast breaks the mana balance.
- The kit: **Basic Vampirism and two Basic Voids** (helmet and weapon), about 6k an hour. The second Void is what pays for the area spells.
- The Wheel: **Front Sweep augment I** first (level 100), then **Combat Mastery** (level 300).
- Each table's **vs no box** column shows the XP change against hunting the same place one monster at a time, where the normal guides rate it comfortable (– if they don't).

## What box hunting means here
- **Two buttons for fighting**, both multi-action buttons (each press casts the first spell that's ready), pressed every 2 seconds (attack spells share a 2-second cooldown), with the auto attack on every turn:
  - **One monster:** `exori ico scu` > `exori ico` > `exori hur`: Shield Bash every 4 seconds, the other two every 8.
  - **Two or more:** `exori scu` > `exori` > `exori min`: each spell about every 6 seconds, in that order. Front Sweep hits only the three squares in front of you, and in a box closing around you that's usually one side, one or two monsters; Shield Slam and Berserk always hit all of them.
  - When the box is down to one monster, go back to the auto attack and the one-monster button.
- **Mana:** with two Voids, the leech from your hits refills what the spells spend, so your mana should stay about level. If it keeps falling, press the area button only on bigger boxes.
- **Healing:** `exura ico` on its own button; `exura gran ico` for emergencies. Healing spells share a 2-second cooldown since June 2026.
- **Pulls:** let the nearest monsters come, up to about 10 squares from where the box starts, up to the size in the Notes (2 to 6); the Notes also show the average box you really get. Sparse hunting grounds give small boxes, and when you clear a round faster than the monsters respawn, fewer are back on the next one: the numbers count both, and only hunting grounds where your boxes average 3 or more, respawn included, are listed. A stronger knight clears faster, so small hunting grounds drop out as you level.
- **Safety:** no listed hunting ground's worst round takes more than about 40% of your health, as in the normal guides. The **Hunt** column grades the attention it needs (0 calm to 3 hard).
- **Keep the shield.** Shield Bash and Shield Slam need one, Battle Healing heals three times as much with one, and shields got 30% more defense in June 2026. A two-handed sword adds only about 6% attack.
- **Stances:** `utamo tempo` (Protector, 15% less damage taken and dealt) when a box goes wrong; `utito tempo` (Blood Rage, +30% melee, 15% more damage taken) for more damage on calm boxes. The numbers count neither.
- **Why not `exori gran`:** in a box, `exori gran` first deals about 60% more damage, but costs about 40% more mana a minute; Shield Slam and Berserk give far more damage per point of mana, so without potions they go further.

## Setting up the character
**Imbuements** (20 hours of fighting each; Market prices from November 2026):

| Item | Imbuement | What it does | Per hour |
|---|---|---|---|
| Armor | Basic Vampirism | 5% life leech | ~1k |
| Helmet | Basic Void (scroll) | 3% mana leech | ~3k |
| Weapon | Basic Void (scroll) | 3% mana leech | ~3k |

Two Voids return twice the mana: that's what pays for the area spells without potions. An area hit leeches less per monster than a single hit, so the auto attack and the one-monster spells do a good share of it.

**Wheel of Destiny** (one point per level from 51; the Hunting Task Shop's 50 points bring each step 50 levels earlier). A slice needs a full neighbour, so the order matters:

| Step | Slices (6 × 6 grid: green top left, red top right, blue bottom left, purple bottom right) | Points | Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Front Sweep augment I (+40% base power) | blue 50 (centre) | 50 | 100 |
| Combat Mastery, stage 1 | + blue top 75 (hit points, +0.25% mana leech) + blue bottom 75 (hit points, gem socket) + 50 in blue bottom 100 | 250 | 300 |
| Fierce Berserk augment I (−30 mana, for later) | + red 50 (centre) | 300 | 350 |
| Front Sweep augment II (+2 squares at your sides) | + red top 75 → red top 100 (+1 melee) → red top 150 (hit points) → red 200 | 825 | 875 |

Combat Mastery adds 1% damage for every 12% of health the target is missing (about 4% over a kill), and less damage taken the lower your own health, doubled with a shield. Front Sweep augment II comes past the guide's levels on this path; it would make Front Sweep hit five squares (a half box).

**Gear, charms, prey, blessings and the Task Board:** as in [Comfortable solo hunting at 100+](comfort_solo_100.md#setting-up-the-character). On the main monster, the minor charms **Void's Call** (mana leech) and **Vampiric Embrace** (life leech) help most here.

**Potions:** none needed on the XP and gold lists' hunting grounds; carry a few of the best health potion your level allows (Great at 80, Ultimate at 130, Supreme at 200) for a bad moment. The **Box with potions** list spends mana and health potions, at most a quarter of the loot (its Supplies/h column).

## Hunting grounds
Numbers per hour of raw experience (no stamina, prey or boosted bonus) and loot at Market prices, with no potions (Box with potions: Supplies/h is the potions at Market prices, Profit/h the loot minus them). XP and loot show the band's first and last level; the rest is for its first level (the imbuements cost about 6k an hour). The XP list takes each hunting ground's pull size with the most XP, the gold list the one with the most loot.

## How the numbers are made
The same fight-by-fight model as the [100+ guide](comfort_solo_100.md#how-the-numbers-are-made), on the same hunting grounds and knight (its gear, skill and the bought level 500+ knight), with these changes:
- **Rotation:** the two buttons above, as often as regeneration and two Basic Voids pay for (as in the normal guides); Front Sweep with augment I from level 100 hits 3 of the 8 squares around you (at least your target), and Combat Mastery adds about 4% from level 300.
- **Mana, checked against tested box hunts:** two knights with two Basic Voids boxed the Forgotten Tomb in Edron, a level 141 one nearly potion-free (1,422 kills and 173k raw XP an hour, 1.3k gold of supplies) and a level 109 one with a few mana potions. The level 109 hunt's server and chat logs showed spells cost what TibiaWiki says, and that the auto attack leeches on every hit, about every 2 seconds for the whole hunt: the model counts that. Mana on kill from weapon proficiency perks (+5 mana a kill in those logs) and other extra leech aren't counted, so a real knight sustains more than these numbers.
- **Pulls:** walking the hunting ground's spawns in order, each box takes the next ones within 10 squares of where it starts (up to 2 to 6, plus any within 3 squares), each monster in one box. If a round takes less time than the monsters need to respawn, only that share is back on the next one, so boxes get smaller and the walk per kill longer; kills an hour never pass the respawn. Only sizes whose boxes average 3 or more, respawn included, count: the one with the most XP and the one with the most loot, both with the worst round (the 90th percentile pull) within 40% of your health.
- **No potions:** a pull size counts only if neither mana nor health needs potions over the hour.
- **Soft hitters:** a box counts only with at least 5 raw XP per point of healing it needs (`exura ico` and health potions, after leech and regeneration). Monsters that die to one or two area casts and hit softly pass easily; big, hard-hitting or self-healing ones don't, because healing them off eats the mana the area spells need. Tested: Forgotten Tomb 43–47 XP per healing point, Edron Vampire Crypt's floor -4 about 6, Caverna Exanima's putrid mummies about 3.
- **Box with potions:** the full rotation, with potions paying what regeneration, leech and `exura ico` don't, while they cost at most 25% of the loot. Each kill takes 1.5x the potion-free fight, walking and looting time, fitted to the one tested potion box hunt (a level 494 knight on Edron Vampire Crypt's floor -4: the model gives 509k raw XP and 31k of potions an hour against the hunt's 501k and 31k).
- **Your shield:** it blocks melee and arrows from two monsters a round, the rest of a box hits in full, and in offensive mode it blocks less than the Cyclopedia's defense value shows (TibiaWiki's formula: about 70%). Shield Bash and Shield Slam halve the next melee hit of every monster they hit.
- **Not counted:** stances, charms, critical hits and weapon proficiency perks. Front Sweep and Shield Slam damage are estimates: Front Sweep scaled from Berserk by base power, Shield Slam from Shield Bash, which has no published formula.

**How far to trust them:** two box hunts are tested so far (above): the model gives 1.03x and 0.81x their kills an hour, and the guides' fight time is fitted to them and nine other tested hunts. The potion list rests on one tested hunt (XP and potions fitted; its loot came in at about half the model's, in a short 19-minute sample). Use the lists to choose where to try first.

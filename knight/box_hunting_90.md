# Box hunting at 90+ (Elite Knight, Nov 2026)

This guide is for a solo Elite Knight from level 90 up who wants **more XP and loot than the no-potion guides**: lure a few monsters together (a **box**), hit them all with area spells on every turn and drink mana potions to keep going. The calm, no-potion way is in the [main knight guide](guide.md) (below 100) and [Comfortable solo hunting at 100+](comfort_solo_100.md); every table here compares against them.

All numbers are **untested estimates** from the same model as those guides, now with potions. Log your box hunts in [spot_tests.md](spot_tests.md).

## The short version
- The best box XP per hour, raw: about **273k at 90, 165k at 100, 770k at 200, 898k at 300, 1.2M at 400, 1.4M at 500, 1.8M at 600, 2.0M at 800**. The best no-potion hunting grounds make about 98k at 90, 101k at 100, 341k at 300, 596k at 500, 817k at 800.
- The listed XP hunting grounds drink about 23k to 315k of potions an hour: up to level 150 nearly all small **Mana Potions** (leech and `exura ico` do the healing), from about 350 up close to half of it health potions, as boxes hit harder. The best gold hunting grounds keep about 637k an hour at level 100 and 826k at 500 after potions.
- The area button: **`exori min` > `exori` > `exori scu`** (Front Sweep, Berserk, Shield Slam). No `exori gran` yet: its 360 mana per cast burns potions fast; it can come later, from about level 300.
- The kit: **Basic Vampirism and two Basic Voids** (helmet and weapon), about 6k an hour.
- The Wheel: **Front Sweep augment I** first (level 100), then **Combat Mastery** (level 300).
- Each table's **vs no potions** column shows the XP change against the same hunting ground hunted without potions, where the no-potion guide rates it comfortable (– if it doesn't).

## What box hunting means here
- **Two buttons for fighting**, both multi-action buttons (each press casts the first spell that's ready), pressed every 2 seconds (attack spells share a 2-second cooldown), with the auto attack on every turn:
  - **One monster:** `exori ico scu` > `exori ico` > `exori hur`: Shield Bash every 4 seconds, the other two every 8.
  - **Two or more:** `exori min` > `exori` > `exori scu`: each spell every 6 seconds. Front Sweep hits only the three squares in front of you, so face the group (the client's Aim at Target option turns you to your target first). With its Wheel augment it's your strongest hit, which is why it goes first.
- **Healing:** `exura ico` on its own button; `exura gran ico` for emergencies. Healing spells share a 2-second cooldown since June 2026.
- **Pulls:** lure the nearest monsters, up to about 10 squares from where the box starts, up to the size in the Notes (2 to 6); the Notes also show the average box you really get. Sparse hunting grounds give small boxes, and when you clear a round faster than the monsters respawn, fewer are back on the next one: the numbers count both, and only hunting grounds where your boxes average 3 or more, respawn included, are listed. A stronger knight clears faster, so small hunting grounds drop out as you level.
- **Mana potions:** put them on one button, smallest first (Mana Potion > Strong > Great): it drinks the smallest you carry. **Mana Potions** (56 gold for about 100 mana, 0.56 gold per point) are the cheapest mana; Strong (108 gold, about 150, level 50) and Great (158 gold, about 200, level 80) cost more per point (0.72 and 0.79) but need fewer drinks. All potions share one cooldown of about a second.
- **Safety:** no listed hunting ground's worst round takes more than about 40% of your health, as in the no-potion guides. The **Hunt** column grades the attention it needs (0 calm to 3 hard).
- **Keep the shield.** Shield Bash and Shield Slam need one, Battle Healing heals three times as much with one, and shields got 30% more defense in June 2026. A two-handed sword adds only about 6% attack.
- **Stances:** `utamo tempo` (Protector, 15% less damage taken and dealt) when a box goes wrong; `utito tempo` (Blood Rage, +30% melee, 15% more damage taken) for more damage on calm boxes. The numbers count neither.
- **Why not `exori gran` first:** as `exori gran` > `exori scu` > `exori min` the spells deal about 25–35% more damage but cost about 55% more mana, and the XP gain is much smaller wherever walking or the respawn is the limit.

## Setting up the character
**Imbuements** (20 hours of fighting each; Market prices from November 2026):

| Item | Imbuement | What it does | Per hour |
|---|---|---|---|
| Armor | Basic Vampirism | 5% life leech | ~1k |
| Helmet | Basic Void (scroll) | 3% mana leech | ~3k |
| Weapon | Basic Void (scroll) | 3% mana leech | ~3k |

Two Voids return twice the mana: with potions paying for the rest, mana leech is what keeps the potion bill down. An area hit leeches less per monster than a single hit, so the auto attack and Shield Bash on one target do a good share of it.

**Wheel of Destiny** (one point per level from 51; the Hunting Task Shop's 50 points bring each step 50 levels earlier). A slice needs a full neighbour, so the order matters:

| Step | Slices (6 × 6 grid: green top left, red top right, blue bottom left, purple bottom right) | Points | Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Front Sweep augment I (+40% base power) | blue 50 (centre) | 50 | 100 |
| Combat Mastery, stage 1 | + blue top 75 (hit points, +0.25% mana leech) + blue bottom 75 (hit points, gem socket) + 50 in blue bottom 100 | 250 | 300 |
| Fierce Berserk augment I (−30 mana, for later) | + red 50 (centre) | 300 | 350 |
| Front Sweep augment II (+2 squares at your sides) | + red top 75 → red top 100 (+1 melee) → red top 150 (hit points) → red 200 | 825 | 875 |

Combat Mastery adds 1% damage for every 12% of health the target is missing (about 4% over a kill), and less damage taken the lower your own health, doubled with a shield. Front Sweep augment II comes past the guide's levels on this path; it would make Front Sweep hit five squares (a half box).

**Gear, charms, prey, blessings and the Task Board:** as in [Comfortable solo hunting at 100+](comfort_solo_100.md#setting-up-the-character). On the main monster, the minor charms **Void's Call** (mana leech) and **Vampiric Embrace** (life leech) help most here.

**Potions:** buy them on the Market first; daily-reward potions often sell far below NPC prices. Carry a few of the best health potion your level allows (Great at 80, Ultimate at 130, Supreme at 200) for a bad moment.

## How the numbers are made
The same fight-by-fight model as the [100+ guide](comfort_solo_100.md#how-the-numbers-are-made), on the same hunting grounds and knight (its gear, skill and the bought level 500+ knight), with these changes:
- **Rotation:** the two buttons above at their full rates; Front Sweep with augment I from level 100 hits the three squares in front, and Combat Mastery adds about 4% from level 300.
- **Pulls:** walking the hunting ground's spawns in order, each box takes the next ones within 10 squares of where it starts (up to 2 to 6, plus any within 3 squares), each monster in one box. If a round takes less time than the monsters need to respawn, only that share is back on the next one, so boxes get smaller and the walk per kill longer; kills an hour never pass the respawn. Only sizes whose boxes average 3 or more, respawn included, count: the one with the most XP and the one with the most profit, both with the worst round (the 90th percentile pull) within 40% of your health.
- **Potions:** the mana that regeneration and two Basic Voids don't cover, and the health that leech and `exura ico` don't, paid with the cheapest pair of potions your level allows whose drinks fit about 0.7 a second over the hour.
- **Not counted:** Shield Slam halving the next hit of every monster it hits (so the damage taken errs high), stances, charms, critical hits and weapon proficiency perks. Front Sweep and Shield Slam damage are estimates: Front Sweep scaled from Berserk by base power, Shield Slam from Shield Bash, which has no published formula.

**How far to trust them:** no box hunt is tested yet; the model is checked against 17 no-potion hunts at levels 19 to 135. Use the lists to choose where to try first.

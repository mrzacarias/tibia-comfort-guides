# Comfortable solo hunting at 100+ (Elite Knight, Nov 2026)

This guide is for a solo Elite Knight from level 100 up who wants **calm hunts with no potions**: auto attack, Shield Bash, `exori` and `exori gran` only as far as the leech from two imbuements pays for them. It assumes you know the game's systems; the [main knight guide](guide.md) explains them from scratch.

The yardstick is a tested hunt: a level 99 knight with Basic Vampirism and Basic Void at the Corym Mines Stonerefiners, about 185k raw XP and 125k loot per hour with no potions; the model is fitted to it. Every hunting ground below is one you can hunt with no potions at all, and each level band unlocks new ones as your level and gear grow.

## The short version
- The best comfortable XP per hour, raw: about **144k at level 100, 357k at 200, 382k at 300, 478k at 400, 851k at 500, 896k at 600 and 999k at 800**. Loot runs from about 402k to 822k per hour at the best gold hunting grounds.
- The kit: **Basic Vampirism and Basic Void** below level 200, about 4k an hour, and **Intricate** from 200, about 27k an hour. Powerful imbuements would cost about 53k an hour (see the comparison at the end).
- Levelling this way gets slow: XP per hour grows much slower than the experience a level needs. Each band's title shows the hours of raw hunting to the next band at the best XP hunting grounds; green stamina, prey and the weekly tasks cut that a lot. Players who report millions of XP per hour box big groups with every area spell and drink potions nonstop.
- Each band starts with the hunting grounds it **unlocks**: comfortable at that level, not at the band before.

## What comfortable means here
- **Rotation:** auto attack and `exori ico scu` (Shield Bash) on your target; on a box of three or more, `exori` and `exori gran` too, but only as often as your mana lasts without potions, healing with `exura ico` first. Void pays for the spells: it returns part of the damage you deal as mana. On the main guide's [multi-action buttons](guide.md#how-to-fight), that's the single-target button, and the area button on boxes while your mana holds.
- **Pulls as they come:** the model walks through each hunting ground and fights each spawn with its neighbours, so pulls of 3 or more happen. Rushing can bring 5 to 8 at once, as at the Stonerefiners; walk at the pace that keeps it calm.
- **Echo Raids:** an echo a creature leaves behind brings 8 more of it, or 4 influenced ones, when you set it off, and now and then a stronger Echo Warden ([main guide](guide.md#bonuses-on-top)). The grades don't count them: walk around echoes (stepping on one sets it off) unless the hunting ground's Hunt grade is 0.
- **Leech does most of the healing**, `exura ico` the rest. The **Hunt** column grades what's left, like the main guide: **0** nothing to watch, **1** a look at your health now and then (the Stonerefiner run was a 1). No listed hunting ground's worst round takes more than the Stonerefiner run's (about 40% of your health).
- **Potions:** none in the numbers: every listed hunting ground is potion-free (under about 300 gold an hour). Carry a health potion for a bad moment anyway; the Potions/h column shows what the model still expects.

## Setting up the character
**Imbuements, at every level** (20 hours of fighting each; Market prices from November 2026):

| Item | Imbuement | What it does | Per hour |
|---|---|---|---|
| Armor | Basic Vampirism (25 Vampire Teeth at a shrine) | 5% life leech | ~1k |
| Helmet | Basic Void (scroll from the Market) | 3% mana leech | ~3k |

Unlock imbuing with the first part of the [Forgotten Knowledge Quest](https://tibia.fandom.com/wiki/Forgotten_Knowledge_Quest) (the main guide's Section 2 explains it). Nothing else is needed for these lists.

**Gear.** Up to 500 the numbers use the main guide's gear for each band (its band 200 gear from 200 on): buy what its gear lines name. From 500 they assume a typical bought level 500 knight from the Char Bazaar: Falcon Plate, Falcon Greaves, Prismatic Helmet, Depth Calcei, Foxtail Amulet, Falcon Shield, melee about 115, and an elemental sword picked per hunting ground (the notes name it): Gnome Sword (energy), Summerblade (fire), Winterblade (ice) or Lion Longsword (earth). Swapping swords splits your weapon proficiency, so keep one main sword. Guardian gems with resistance mods (about 6% against each element with four lesser and four regular gems) come with most such knights.

**Charms.** Points grow with your Bestiary; spend them in this order:
1. **Dodge** (240 points, 600 for stage 2: 5% then 10% chance to take no damage) on the monster that hurts you most.
2. **Wound** (same cost), or the elemental charm the main monster is weak to: 10% of your attacks deal 5% of its maximum health at stage 2. On big monsters this is the largest damage boost you can buy.
3. Minor charms **Vampiric Embrace** (+2.4% life leech) and **Void's Call** (+1.2% mana leech) on the main monster: they work with Basic leech too, and nearly double it.
4. **Parry** later; **Low Blow** and **Savage Blow** only with a Strike imbuement.

**Wheel of Destiny.** One point per level from 51, plus up to 50 from the Hunting Task Shop (the first 10 cost only 17,500 Hunting Task Points). Fill the hit-point slices first, then **Battle Instinct** and **Battle Healing** (healing spells 10% stronger each, three times that with a shield). At 300 points in one domain (around level 350) take the **Combat Mastery** revelation perk: less damage the lower your health, doubled with a shield. Plan it at tibiapal.com/wheels.

**Weapon proficiency.** Keep one main weapon per band; the first four levels come in a few hours of hunting, and you pick damage or leech perks.

**Prey.** Damage reduction on the hardest hitter and bonus experience on the main monster, as in the main guide.

**Task Board.** Wear a [Bounty Talisman](guide.md#task-board-bounty-and-weekly-tasks) and take bounty creatures from your hunting ground. A weekly task gives 1,994 × your level in XP, capped by the difficulty: **Beginner** (200k) is enough to level 100, **Adept** (800k) to about level 400, **Expert** (3 million) above that. Twelve weekly tasks are 4.8M XP at 200 and 12.0M at 500, a big share of a week's levelling at this pace.

**Before each hunt.**
- **Blessings:** all seven plus Twist of Fate cost about 138k at level 100, 234k at 200, 306k at 300 and 420k at 500 (or 130 Tibia Coins for the seven in the Store).
- **Potions:** small **Mana Potions** (56 gold for 75–125 mana) are the cheapest mana per gold. For emergencies carry the best health potion your level allows: Great (level 80), Ultimate (130) or Supreme (200). Great Mana Potions (knights too since June 2026, about 200 mana for 158 gold) save potion turns in the harder bands. Buy potions on the Market first: daily-reward potions often sell there far below NPC prices.
- **Healing spells:** `exura ico` for top-ups, `exura med ico` from level 300, `exura gran ico` (level 80) for emergencies, `utamo tempo` (Protector) when a pull grows.
- **The Forge:** skip fusing. It works half the time, and a failure costs the second copy a tier, or destroys it at tier 0. If you want a tiered item, buy one already made on the Market; to keep a tier when you change items, **Transfer** always works (the new item gets the tier minus one; see the [main guide](guide.md#section-6-the-forge-and-the-full-elite-knight)). Dust you don't need sells as Slivers or Exalted Cores.

## How the numbers are made
The same fight-by-fight model as the [main guide](guide.md#appendix-how-the-numbers-are-made), on its hunting grounds and every other TibiaWiki hunting place with coordinates:
- **You:** up to 500, the main guide's typical knight (its gear, melee about 81 at level 100, 97 at 200, 100 at 300, 103 at 400); from 500, the bought knight above (melee 115 at 500, 117 at 600, 121 at 800). Basic Vampirism and Void below 200, Intricate from 200, and half the Wheel's points in hit-point slices.
- **Damage:** TibiaWiki's attack formula; auto attack, and Shield Bash, `exori` and `exori gran` at the share of their full rates your mana pays for without potions (regeneration plus Void, with the lower leech of area hits); each spawn is pulled with the spawns within 3 tiles of it (up to 8), and every monster in the hunting ground counts, also the ones too big to be worth killing.
- **The yardstick in the same terms:** the model's fight time is fitted so the Stonerefiner run's knight (club 107) makes about 370 kills an hour with no potions, as the real hunt did.
- **Monsters, spawns and loot** from TibiaWiki, the Canary open-source server and Market prices of three big worlds (November 2026); drops rarer than about once in 3 hours left out.

**How far to trust them:** checked against 17 real hunts at levels 19 to 135, none above that yet. The model doesn't know charms, weapon proficiency perks, the Wheel's damage bonuses or critical hits, so real XP should come out higher, and a knight with more skill than the typical one (heavy offline training) does better. Monsters with special mechanics are judged only by their damage. Use the lists to choose where to try first, and log your hunts in [spot_tests.md](spot_tests.md).

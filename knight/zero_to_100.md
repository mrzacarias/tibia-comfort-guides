# Comfortably raising a knight from 0 to 100

This guide takes a new knight from character creation to level 100 as fast as comfort allows, and shows what the character is worth on the Char Bazaar at the end. It builds on the [knight guide](guide.md): the same comfort grades (only hunting grounds with Prep and Hunt of 0 or 1), the same hunting grounds and the same model. It assumes a premium account. Every number is an estimate; the last part explains how they're made.

**In short:** hunt 3 hours a day and train offline 12 hours a day. Go through Targuna, then Corym Mines with a Stealth Ring, and hand in only the cheap delivery tasks. That's level 100 in about **28 days** (84 hours of hunting), with sword about 78. A knight like that has sold for a median of about **259 TC**.

## What the plan assumes
- **3 hours of hunting a day.** They're all in green stamina (+50% experience): stamina refills offline, and 21 hours offline a day is more than the 18 hours its last 3 hours need.
- **12 hours of offline training every day**, at a Training Statue with Sword Fighting + Shielding. Your training time refills one second for every second you're online or offline without training, so 3 hours online plus 9 hours offline gives the next day's 12 hours back.
- **Skill only from offline training.** Hunting adds a little more, so the real skill will be a bit higher.
- **No deaths, no bought experience boosts and no events.** Deaths make it slower; events and boosts make it faster.
- **The hunting grounds come from the knight guide's Good for XP lists** (Section 1, and Section 1a with the ring): each day, the best one for your level and skill.

## Day 0: create the character
1. **Pick the world first:** a world with Optional PvP and green BattlEye, ideally in North America. It decides the price later (see "Selling the character"), and moving later costs a world transfer.
2. **On Newhaven, the starting island**, do the [Newhaven Quest](https://tibia.fandom.com/wiki/Newhaven_Quest) (1,600 experience and a Guardian Ring) and the [Newhaven Legion Helmet Quest](https://tibia.fandom.com/wiki/Newhaven_Legion_Helmet_Quest) before you leave. You can't go back.
3. **On Targuna, at level 8, train offline first:** use the Training Statue and log out for 12 hours. That takes sword from about 16 to 43 and shielding from 12 to 36, which more than doubles your damage.

## Weekly delivery tasks: only the cheap ones
The **Task Board** gives each character 6 delivery tasks and 6 kill tasks a week, reset on Monday at server save (9 of each with the Permanent Weekly Task Expansion, 750 TC in the Store). A delivery task gives **half a level of experience up to level 82**, and 1,994 × your level after that. Kill tasks give the same, with a cap (200k experience for beginner tasks, 800k for adept).

The catch is the items. Each delivery asks for 5 to 500 of an item drawn from 484 possible ones, mostly creature products, some armors and weapons. At Market prices the middle amount costs about 191k gold for half of them, and only 5% cost less than 20k. At low levels, half a level is worth little, so most deliveries cost more than the hunting they save.

The rule: **hand in a delivery only when its items cost less than what you'd loot in the time its experience saves.** On the ring route, that means:

| Level | Experience per delivery | Hunting time it replaces | Worth paying up to | Tasks that cheap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30 | 20k | 9 min | 6k | 0% |
| 40 | 37k | 17 min | 15k | 2% |
| 50 | 59k | 23 min | 30k | 10% |
| 60 | 86k | 33 min | 47k | 21% |
| 70 | 117k | 39 min | 61k | 28% |
| 80 | 154k | 45 min | 80k | 34% |
| 90 | 179k | 53 min | 93k | 36% |
| 100 | 199k | 57 min | 99k | 37% |

- **Deliver on Sunday,** the last day before the reset: the experience depends on your level when you hand it in, so later pays more.
- **Keep what your hunting ground drops** if one of the week's deliveries asks for it: that one is free.
- **Check the price on your world's Market** before buying; it can be very different from the numbers above (Antica, November 2026).
- **Kill tasks** are worth doing when the creature lives where you already hunt (a Stonerefiner task is free experience). Going somewhere else for one costs about as much hunting time as the experience it gives. The timeline below doesn't count them.

## Prey: why it barely helps here
The prey system (Ctrl+Y) gives a 2-hour bonus against one creature: a damage boost (7–25%), damage reduction (12–30%), bonus experience (13–40%) or improved loot (13–40%). Premium accounts have 2 slots. Each slot offers 9 random creatures; the list rerolls for free every 20 hours, or for 150 gold × your level. The bonus type is random when you pick.

On this route it doesn't pay:
- **Stonerefiners never appear in the random lists.** They're one of the creatures you can only pick with 5 **Prey Wildcards** ("select a creature"), and that lasts 2 hours. Locking a creature and its bonus also costs 5 wildcards.
- **Wildcards are scarce:** a premium account gets 3 a week from the daily reward (day 3 of the reward lane). The Store sells 5 for 50 TC, which is more than the character earns in those 2 hours.
- **Damage reduction is useless with the ring,** since the Stonerefiners never hit you. The Infernoids of the Crimson Court can't be picked at all.

So: save the 3 free wildcards each week. Every second week, spend 5 on Stonerefiner. If the bonus isn't experience, one more wildcard rerolls it: it always raises the bonus a step and may change its type (at the top step, it always changes). That's 2 hours of +13–40% experience every two weeks, a small extra the timeline doesn't count.

## Other free boosts
- **Daily reward:** day 7 of the reward lane gives 30 minutes of 50% more experience. Use it on a Corym Mines day. Collecting every day also gives stamina regeneration in depots and temples.
- **Boosted creature:** if the day's boosted creature lives on your route, hunt it that day (twice the experience and loot).
- **Double experience events** happen a few times a year and speed everything up a lot.
- **Not worth it on this route:** imbuements and the Wheel of Destiny (you take almost no damage with the ring), charms (a few percent of damage) and the Store's XP Boost (it costs more Tibia Coins than the character earns while it lasts).

## Selling the character
On the [Char Bazaar](https://www.tibia.com/charactertrade/?subtopic=currentcharactertrades) the seller pays a 50 TC fee to list (lost if the character doesn't sell) and 12% of the price. An account can have one auction at a time, and the coins from a sale may arrive as non-transferable.

What knights like this sold for (sold auctions on exiva.pro, levels within 10% and sword within 2, all worlds):

| Level | Sword | Sales | Median | Middle half |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 56 (end of week 1) | 64 | 19 | 200 TC | 66–266 |
| 74 (end of week 2) | 71 | 31 | 157 TC | 100–400 |
| 88 (end of week 3) | 75 | 53 | 151 TC | 100–250 |
| 100 (end of week 4) | 78 | 50 | 259 TC | 115–600 |

The price doesn't grow from level 50 to about 85, then rises about 1.6× by level 100. **Sell right after level 100**, not on the way.

The world matters as much as the level. Each sale's price compared with the median for its level and skill (238 sales):

**Create the character on a world with Optional PvP and green BattlEye, ideally in North America.** Never yellow BattlEye. Also end the auction in the evening of the world's region, not in the hours just before server save, when prices are lowest.

TC per character = 0.88 × the median price − the 50 TC fee (counted for 1 in 5 auctions not selling) + the gold left, at 43,000 gold per Tibia Coin. At 3 hours a day that's about **349 TC a month**, and about 412 on an Optional PvP, green world (×1.25).

Not counted:
- **Premium time.** If you keep premium only for this, subtract it.
- **Gear.** Sell it before listing.
- **Deaths.**

Prices scatter a lot: half of the sales fall in a range 2 to 5 times wide. Sold characters often carry extras a fresh one doesn't have (outfits, mounts, charms). Treat these numbers as a rough guide.

## How the numbers are made
- **The route:** a day-by-day simulation with the knight guide's model (its appendix) at the character's real level and skill. XP × the low level bonus × green stamina; skill from offline training only, with TibiaWiki's formulas.
- **Delivery tasks:** TibiaWiki's Delivery Task list, with Market prices from tibiamarket.top (Antica, November 2026).
- **Prices:** sold auctions on exiva.pro.
